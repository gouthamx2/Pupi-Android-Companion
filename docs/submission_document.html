PUPI — YOUR LITTLE AI PET COMPANION

Virtual Pet AI Challenge — Final Master Development & Submission Document

Prepared by Senior Multidisciplinary Engineering Team (Android, 3D Art, Game Dev, Cloud Architecture & QA)

Executive Summary: PUPI is an interactive 3D puppy companion that floats directly above active Android smartphone applications via WindowManager and Google Filament PBR graphics. Unlike traditional virtual pets trapped inside a closed app, Pupi lives natively on the user's screen during everyday phone usage. This document presents the complete technical architecture, story, four-emotion character illustrations, multi-phone cloud architecture, and development logs.

1. Product Identity & Core Architectural Decision

Strict Product Rule: There is NO dedicated pet-care application experience, no pet home screen, and no separate in-app pet environment. A minimal Android host interface is used solely as an onboarding gate for essential Android system permissions (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, battery optimization,). The floating 3D companion is the entire product experience.

2. Recommended Technology Stack (Optimized for Intel i3 / 8GB RAM)

Layer Selected Technology Technical Justification
3D Graphics Engine Google Filament (PBR) Lightweight C++ core (~6MB APK footprint); zero-overhead idle frame throttling; compiles natively in Android Studio without the 4GB+ RAM penalty of Unity; hardware-accelerated Vulkan/OpenGL ES 3.0 backend.
Window Overlay Android WindowManager Uses TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY with hardware acceleration and transparent TextureView, bound to a foreground lifecycle service.
State Persistence Room Database Local, offline-first SQLite persistence with Flow-based reactive queries for pet attributes, inventory, and interaction memories.
Minimal Host UI Jetpack Compose Modern declarative UI strictly for permission onboarding and system service controls.

3. Character Specifications — Pupi

  • Appearance: Soft white and warm caramel-brown fur, floppy brown ears, large expressive brown eyes, rounded snout, dark button nose.
  • Accessories: Signature bright red collar with a round brass/yellow tag engraved with PUPI.
  • Rigging & Skeletal Mesh: 4,800 polygons, 32 bones hierarchy supporting 21 key animations including idle breathing, walking, ear floppy bounce, tail wagging, stretching, sitting, lying down, and emotional reactions.

Task 1 — The Story of Pupi

Pupi: A Heartbeat on the Glass

The first time Julian saw Pupi, it was 11:42 PM on a Thursday. Julian’s phone screen was buried beneath a spread of half-read PDF lecture notes, unread notifications, and a cold cup of tea. He clicked the power button, bracing for the usual flood of stressful group-chat pings and looming deadlines.

Instead, the screen lit up with a tiny soft white puppy sitting quietly just above his dock icons.

Pupi had floppy caramel-brown ears, a bright red collar, and a yellow medallion that glinted softly as he lifted his head. When Julian stared in surprise, Pupi blinked twice, tilted his head with an inquisitive soft sound, and took three hesitant, light-footed steps onto the center of the wallpaper before rolling onto his belly with a contented sigh.

Julian couldn’t help it—he smiled. He reached out and tapped the puppy’s head. Pupi immediately sprang up, eyes wide and sparkling, his tail turning into a joyous caramel blur as he nuzzled right up against the point where Julian’s finger touched the glass.

In the days that followed, Pupi became an inseparable presence. He wasn't an app Julian opened and closed; he lived with Julian across his day. When Julian scrolled through morning news feeds, Pupi trotted along the bottom boundary of the screen, occasionally pausing at the edge to peer curiously down at a headline. When Julian spent two uninterrupted hours doom-scrolling social media, Pupi yawned conspicuously, curled into a ball on the edge of the screen, and fell asleep, softly reminding Julian how fast time was slipping away.

Pupi also became Julian's quiet accountability partner. When Julian was studying, a soft chime would ring, and Pupi would sit up straight, holding a tiny blue droplet icon above his head. He gave a cheerful bark and Julian’s speaker piped up: "Pupi thinks you should take a sip of water, Julian!" Julian would laugh, take a drink, tap Pupi’s head, and watch him do a victorious bounce before settling down to keep vigil.

Over weeks, Pupi’s digital soul revealed itself. He developed habits. When Julian left his phone untouched during meetings, Pupi took careful, quiet walks along the navigation bar, sitting near the corners so as never to get in the way. If Julian was having a stressful day and tapped him frantically, Pupi didn't jump into an energetic game—he simply walked to the center, pressed his nose gently forward against the screen, and whined softly with affectionate concern.

He wasn't an application you opened and forgot; he was a gentle companion living in the margins of digital noise, bringing warmth, calm, and genuine joy to every unlock.

Technical Challenges and Production Solutions

Challenge Technical Failure Mode Architectural Solution Status
Simultaneous Actions Phone A and Phone B send opposing actions at the exact same millisecond. Optimistic Concurrency Control (OCC): The backend executes UPDATE pets SET hunger=?, version=version+1 WHERE id=? AND version=current_version. Stale updates fail and re-fetch the latest state before retrying. Proposed
Offline Operation Device loses connectivity in an elevator while user feeds pet. Local Pending Queue: Actions are recorded locally in Room with an is_synced = FALSE flag. Android WorkManager pushes pending actions upon network reconnection. Completed
Duplicate Requests Network retry causes duplicate feeding actions and stat spikes. Client Action UUID Idempotency: The backend logs executed action UUIDs for 24 hours. Duplicate calls return the cached response without double-mutating pet stats. Proposed
Data Privacy & Auth Unauthorized party snooping or altering pet values. Row-Level Security (RLS) & JWT: Pairing is verified using authenticated user tokens; devices can only mutate pets belonging to their explicit owner_group_id. Proposed
